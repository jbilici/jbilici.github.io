Pipe Trades Pro alternatives without a subscription (iPhone, 2026)

iPhone pipefitter calculators you pay for once, or not at all, compared with the Pipe Trades Pro Calc subscription: Pipe Calc Elite, PipeBook, Cutr and others, with prices, features and update dates.

Updated October 18, 2026 · by Jiyan Bilici

Disclosure: we make Cutr, one of the options below. Facts are from each app's US App Store listing on October 18, 2026 (linked); check the listing before you buy, since prices change.

Why people look for an alternative

Pipe Trades Pro Calc by Calculated Industries is the best-known pipe trades calculator on the iPhone (4,373 ratings, 4.8★). It is free to try, then an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year on the US App Store. Over three years the yearly plan comes to about $120. Its last update was on July 11, 2025.

It is a good app, and if you need its flow, pressure-loss and pipe-data tools it is hard to replace. But many fitters only use a few functions (offsets, take-outs, cut lengths) and would rather pay once. These are the iPhone options that do that.

Closest match: Pipe Calc Elite

If you like the Pipe Trades Pro calculator keypad, Pipe Calc Elite is the nearest one-purchase equivalent we found: simple and rolling offsets, take-outs and cut lengths, thread make-up, pipe data for seven materials up to 42", pressure loss, conversions and a feet-inch-fraction calculator, with the manual built in. It has been on the App Store since 2013 (168 ratings, 4.8★) and its listing says it is not affiliated with Calculated Industries.

Tables plus calculators: PipeBook

PipeBook ($12.99 once, released August 2026) leans toward reference: pipe schedules, B16.9 take-offs and B16.5 flanges with the full bolt-up, plus rolling offset, cut-a-fitting, miter, parallel offset and weight calculators. It is new, so it has few ratings yet (10).

Free core, one-time Pro: Cutr

Cutr is our app and is not on the App Store yet. Offsets with travel, run and cut length, and the butt-weld, socket-weld and threaded take-out tables are free with no time limit. Cutr Pro is a single $6.99 purchase for rolling offsets, equal-spread offsets, flanges and valves, stud bolts, miters, saddle templates and laterals. Every answer shows a drawing with your numbers and the formula used. It does not do flow, pressure loss or pipe weights, so it doesn't replace that part of Pipe Trades Pro.

Which one should you pick?

  • You use Pipe Trades Pro for everything, including flow and pressure: stay with it, or try Pipe Calc Elite.
  • You want tables and calculators in one paid app: PipeBook.
  • You mostly do offsets, take-outs and cut lengths: start with a free option (Cutr's free tier) and pay only if you need more.
  • Whatever you pick, check one offset and one take-out against a number you trust (your fitting catalog, ASME B16.9 or the Blue Book) before cutting.

FAQ

Is there a pipefitter app without a subscription?

Yes. Pipe Calc Elite ($19.99), PipeBook ($12.99) and LMF's Pipe Fitter Calculator ($4.99) are one-time purchases, Pipedata is free, and Cutr is free with a one-time $6.99 Pro unlock.

How much does Pipe Trades Pro cost?

On the US App Store on October 18, 2026: a free trial, then $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Calculated Industries also sells the handheld Pipe Trades Pro calculator separately.

Do these apps work offline?

Pipe Trades Pro, Pipe Calc Elite, PipeBook and Cutr all say they work without a connection.
