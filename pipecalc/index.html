Pipe Offset Calculator: Fitter

Rolling offset, miter, saddle

Coming soon to the App Store

All the pipe math in one app. Pay once, no subscription, no ads, no account. Every answer comes with a drawing of the fitting, labelled with your numbers, so you can check it before you cut.

What it does

  • Simple offsets: travel, run and cut length for any fitting angle (11¼°, 22½°, 45°, 60° or custom)
  • Rolling offsets: true offset, travel, run and cut from roll and set
  • Equal-spread and parallel offsets
  • Take-outs for elbows, tees and reducers, with your own values when your fittings differ
  • Miter cuts: pieces, cut angle and cutback
  • Saddle and branch layout ordinates for wrap-around templates
  • Type 3' 7 5/16" the way you say it, or work in millimetres
  • Round to 1/16" or 1/32"
  • Every result shows the formula and the inputs it used
  • History of your last calculations, all offline
